# Global HR Policy Handbook

*Document owner: Global People Operations. Effective date: 1 January 2025. Version: 4.2.*

## Purpose and Scope

This handbook describes the employment policies that apply to every employee, regardless of location, grade, or function. It covers working hours, leave, parental support, performance management, compensation support, probation, and notice periods. Contractors and agency workers are governed by their statements of work, although the conduct and security expectations in this handbook apply to them while they work on company premises or systems. Where local law or a collective agreement is more favourable, it prevails. Managers are accountable for applying these rules consistently, and any deviation requires prior written approval from the Chief People Officer and is recorded in the HR system for audit purposes.

## Working Hours and Remote Work

The standard work week is 40 hours, scheduled around the needs of the team and its customers. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval, requested at least five business days in advance through the HR system. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in the employee's local time zone; employees must be reachable and available for meetings during those hours. Time outside core hours may be arranged flexibly with the manager. Remote employees must have a private space, a reliable connection, and the security controls described in the IT Security and Access Policy. A manager may withdraw a remote-work arrangement with 30 days' written notice. Fully remote contracts require approval from the Chief People Officer and are reviewed annually.

## Annual Leave

Employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, prorated for new joiners based on the number of complete months remaining in the year. A maximum of 5 unused days may be carried over into the following year and must be used by 31 March of the following year; any leave still unused after that date is forfeited without payment unless local law requires otherwise. Requests should be submitted at least 10 business days before the first day of absence, and any request covering more than 10 consecutive working days requires director approval. Leave balances are visible in the HR system and reviewed quarterly by People Operations.

## Sick Leave and Medical Certification

Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. On the first day of absence, the employee must notify their manager and update their HR status before 10:00 local time. A medical certificate is required for any absence longer than 3 consecutive working days, and it must be uploaded within five business days of returning to work. Sick leave does not carry over between years and is not converted to cash on termination. Absences related to a chronic condition or a longer-term illness are managed as a reasonable-accommodation case with the support of People Operations. Managers must not ask for clinical details and should refer fitness-for-work questions to occupational health.

## Parental Leave

Parental leave is available to all employees with at least 12 months of continuous service. The primary caregiver is entitled to 26 weeks fully paid, and the secondary caregiver is entitled to 4 weeks fully paid. Leave may be taken as one continuous block beginning no earlier than two weeks before the expected date of birth or placement and ending no later than 12 months after that date. Employees must give at least eight weeks' written notice, and People Operations confirms the dates and pay arrangements in writing. Pension contributions and health benefits continue during paid leave. Employees returning from parental leave are entitled to the same role or an equivalent one, and a return-to-work plan is agreed with the manager before the return date.

## Performance Management

Formal performance reviews take place twice a year, in April and October. The April cycle confirms the previous year's rating, sets objectives for the year ahead, and is linked to the annual compensation review. The October cycle is a mid-year review that tracks progress against those objectives and adjusts them if business priorities have changed. Every employee must have a documented one-to-one conversation with their manager each month and must receive written peer feedback in both cycles. An employee whose performance falls below expectations is placed on a written performance improvement plan with clear milestones, weekly check-ins, and a review at the end of the plan period; the plan runs for a minimum of 60 days.

## Compensation, Referral Bonus, and Remote Work Allowance

Salaries are reviewed once a year in April, alongside the performance review cycle, and any change takes effect on 1 April. Employees who refer a candidate for a permanent role receive a referral bonus of USD 1,500, paid in the payroll cycle after the referred hire completes 90 days of employment. The bonus is taxable and is not available to managers referring candidates into their own team or to employees in the recruiting function. Employees approved for remote work may claim home internet reimbursement of USD 50 per month, submitted monthly with a copy of the service provider invoice. Reimbursement covers connectivity only.

## Probation and Notice Periods

New employees serve a probation period of 3 months for individual contributors and 6 months for managers. During probation, either party may end the employment with 15 days' written notice, and probation may be extended once by up to three months where more time is needed to assess performance. After probation, the notice period is 60 days for individual contributors (engineers) and 90 days for people managers, unless a local contract or law sets a longer period. Notice must be given in writing and runs from the first working day after it is received. The company may place an employee on garden leave and may pay in lieu of notice only where local law permits. All company property must be returned by the last working day.

## Policy Review and Governance

People Operations reviews this handbook every 12 months with Legal and the regional HR leads. Material changes are communicated to all employees at least 30 days before they take effect, and the current version is always available on the intranet. Employees are responsible for reading the handbook and for asking questions when a rule is unclear. Questions about leave, probation, or notice go to the HR service desk at people@company.example; questions about local law go to the regional HR business partner. Managers who approve an exception must document the business rationale; undocumented exceptions are treated as policy breaches during internal audit.
